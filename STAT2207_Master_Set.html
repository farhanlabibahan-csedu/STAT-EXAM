(a)

(i) Repetition allowed, leading zeros allowed (Multiplication Rule): 10 choices for each of 4 positions.
N = 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 104 = 10,000 PINs.

(ii) All 4 digits distinct (Permutation Rule): 10 choices for 1st, 9 for 2nd, 8 for 3rd, 7 for 4th.

(iii) Starts with 7, ends with an even digit (repetition allowed):

(b)

Let D = person has the disease. Prior: P(D) = 0.02, P(D̄) = 0.98.

Here σ = 12, n = 36, so SE = 12 / √36 = 126 = 2.000.
